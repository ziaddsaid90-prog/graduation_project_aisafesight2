3. Method
---------
Person and truck boxes from the pretrained COCO YOLO11l were checked on every frame and kept where tight.
Helmet, vest and wheelbarrow boxes were placed by hand on zoomed crops around the worker in each frame.

3b. Chain barrier (classes 8/9, added 2026-11-10)
-------------------------------------------------
One box per frame around the whole barrier: both posts and the chain between them.
chain_closed = chain hangs between the posts across the entrance. chain_open = chain lowered to the ground.
Timeline: closed 0-3.5 s -> lowered by 4.0 s -> open while the worker goes to the trucks and back ->
worker re-hooks it at ~13.5-14 s -> closed 14-15 s.
