3. Method
---------
- A pretrained COCO YOLO11l was run only to propose person/vehicle locations. COCO has no wheelbarrow, helmet or vest
  classes, and it misread most wheelbarrows as bicycles/motorcycles, so no prediction was accepted as-is.
- Every box was placed by hand on a coordinate grid, drawn back on the image, inspected, and tightened.
  Small people and PPE were placed on zoomed crops of the full-resolution photos (4096 px site images).
- CVAT was not available in this session, so review was done on rendered overlays instead.

4. Decisions applied (please confirm or change)
----------------------------------------------
a) Bicycles, motorcycles and e-scooters are NOT labelled vehicle_other (images 100, 101, 106, 107, 109 contain
   parked ones). CONFIRMED by the project owner on 2026-11-10: two-wheelers stay unlabelled.
   Apply the same rule to the mixer and framing sources so the classes stay consistent.
b) Powered barrows (motorised one-axle carts) are labelled wheelbarrow: 110, 140, 169, 178, 19.
   README defines wheelbarrow as "manual wheelbarrow or two-wheel concrete cart".
c) Pedestrians in 100.jpg are labelled person + no_helmet + no_vest (heads and torsos clearly visible).
d) A cap or soft hat is not a helmet: 179.jpg woman in a pink cap -> no_helmet.
e) Wheelbarrow boxes cover the barrow itself (tray, frame, wheel, handles), not loose cargo such as hoses,
   shovels or machines sitting on top.
f) No helmet/no_helmet label when the head is outside the frame (131, 139, 163). No vest/no_vest label when the
   torso is mostly outside the frame (139).
g) 18.jpg: the drum concrete mixer at the right edge is a stationary site mixer, not a truck. It is left
   unlabelled (no matching class).
