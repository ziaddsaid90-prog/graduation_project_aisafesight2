3. Method
---------
Same as barwita: a pretrained COCO YOLO11l proposed person/truck/car boxes; every box was checked on a grid overlay,
corrected or redrawn by hand, and small people/PPE were placed on zoomed crops. COCO "truck" was never mapped
automatically: each truck was looked at and labelled mixer_truck only if a mixing drum is visible.

4. Decisions applied
--------------------
a) vehicle_other: cars, vans, dump trucks, excavators, loaders, concrete pump trucks, cranes on wheels.
   Motorcycles/scooters NOT labelled (13.jpg), as confirmed for barwita.
b) 24.jpg: the large truck in front is a plain truck head (no drum visible) -> vehicle_other, not mixer_truck.
c) A hi-vis jacket, coverall or shirt counts as vest (orange/yellow with reflective strips), as in the README.
d) Drivers seen through a cab window are NOT labelled (4, 27): less than ~30% visible.
e) A white/plain hard hat counts as helmet; a cap or bare head = no_helmet only when the head is clearly visible.
